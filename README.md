This repository is for my MSc Res Thesis titled 'The Effect of Fluid Chemistry on the Dynamics of Friction Evolution during Early Fault Development'. 
Friction data is for the dynamic experiments (Table. 4 in thesis). 
Stick-slip data is for the dynamic experiments (Table. 4 in thesis) - details of stick-slip method included in section 2.2.1 Friction Data. 
Geotribology 'How To' file is an unofficial document that I put together for two mechanical engineering interns over the summer who were looking to repeat the methodology.
